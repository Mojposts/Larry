# Saunders Farm – Reporting Fix Plan (Fall 2026)

_Updated October 29, 2026 · Owner: Mohamed Jama_

## What happened

While building the Christmas plan, Richard found that our weekly Fright Fest and Pumpkin Fest reports show **media spend** (what Meta and Google charge) rather than **client cost** (what Saunders is billed). ROAS and CPA were calculated on media spend too, even where the reports label them as client cost-based. A few copy-paste errors and mismatched labels also made it into the live reports, and the 2026 spend rows in the client's sales sheet came from these reports.

**Not affected:** billing and pacing (paced against the $105K IO), the warehouse, the Christmas plan, and platform conversion and revenue numbers.

Full details: [Saunders Reporting Issues 2026](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1lPC-V203Tu7AbSNcqYh3SfMxI0GBRYMpla7Czq_A48U/edit)
